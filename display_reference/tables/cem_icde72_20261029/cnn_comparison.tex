Across the three LOS tasks, CEM-Net exceeds TCN, ModernTCN and LITEMV by 4.89--8.90 AP percentage points and Medformer by 3.36--6.28 points (Table~\ref{tab:primary}). All corresponding family-adjusted intervals are positive (Holm $p\leq 0.0015$ for the CNNs and $p\leq 0.0003$ for Medformer). The corresponding mortality intervals are also positive in all three databases. Mean AUROC and Brier score also favor CEM-Net throughout these comparisons. For eICU mortality, however, mean log loss is lower for LITEMV (0.1593) and Medformer (0.1627) than for CEM-Net (0.1647).
