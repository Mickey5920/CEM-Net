Across the three LOS tasks, CEM-Net exceeds TCN, ModernTCN and LITEMV by 4.89--8.90 AP percentage points and Medformer by 3.36--6.28 points (Table~\ref{tab:primary}). All corresponding family-adjusted intervals are positive (Holm $p\leq 0.0015$ for the CNNs and $p\leq 0.0003$ for Medformer). Mean AUROC and Brier score also favor CEM-Net throughout these LOS comparisons.
